A SPECIAL INVITATION

With Love, We Welcome You

A beautiful day of love, family, tradition and togetherness.

Siyakwamukela

There are some moments in life that deserve to be celebrated slowly, beautifully, and surrounded by the people who matter most.

This is one of those moments.

With hearts full of gratitude and excitement, we invite you to join us for a beautiful Umembeso — a celebration of love, family, tradition and new beginnings.

It is a day for families to come together, for beautiful traditions to be honoured, and for memories to be created in the company of the people we hold closest to our hearts.

Your presence would make this beautiful day even more meaningful.

With love, Sphamandla & Nhlakanipho
